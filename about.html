About the initiative

Who we are.

We contribute to mathematical research and education. Our research sits in applied mathematics — infectious disease modelling, nonlinear partial differential equations, and neural network applications.

Advancing mathematical knowledge.

Research Areas

Where our work is focused
Infectious Disease Modelling
Mathematical modelling of infectious diseases, from transmission dynamics to inference about how an outbreak is likely to unfold.
Nonlinear PDEs
Applications of nonlinear partial differential equations.
Neural Network Applications
Applications of neural networks, currently focused on inference problems in infectious disease modelling.

Non-Profit Activity

Education & outreach

Math Olympiad Education

We teach Mathematical Olympiad training to young Indonesian students, for those with minimal or no prior olympiad experience, to build competitiveness and encourage achievement.
